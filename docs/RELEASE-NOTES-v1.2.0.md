# AuraScribe v1.2.0 — Your Year recap + shareable cards

Still free, open source, and **100% offline** (the one-time model download remains the only network
request in the whole app). This release completes the Insights page.

## What's new

- **"Your Year" recap.** A yearly review of your dictation — the hours you saved by speaking instead
  of typing, total words, dictations, active days, your speaking rate, and your busiest day. Open it
  any time from the Insights page; around year-end (December–January) it also gets its own sidebar
  entry, then recedes.
- **Shareable cards.** Turn your streak or your yearly recap into a clean image you can post anywhere.
  "Share as image" renders the card **on your device** and saves it to your Pictures folder — nothing
  is uploaded; you decide where it goes.

Everything is computed from your existing local history. No new data is collected, and the strict
offline policy is unchanged.

## Install

Download `AuraScribe_1.2.0_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. Windows 10/11, ~9 MB, no account, no
subscription. A first run downloads your chosen voice model once; everything after that is offline.
